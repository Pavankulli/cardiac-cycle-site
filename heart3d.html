The cardiac cycle: left heart

A 3D heart in anatomical position, cut open through all four chambers. The heart is drawn in anatomical position with all four chambers; only the left heart (LA, LV, mitral and aortic valves, aorta) is simulated, so the right atrium, right ventricle and their valves are drawn still. Proportions are approximate. Every wall, valve, pressure and sound is driven by one time-varying-elastance model of the left heart.

Teaching model, not a patient simulator: lumped left heart (time-varying elastance LV and LA, non-return valves, windkessel aorta, venous return). Normal at 75 bpm: EDV ≈ 120 mL, ESV ≈ 50 mL, SV ≈ 70 mL, BP ≈ 120/80. The ECG is synthetic and timed to the model's activation.

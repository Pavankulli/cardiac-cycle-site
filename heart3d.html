Pressure–volume loop

Teaching model, not a patient simulator: lumped left heart (time-varying elastance LV and LA, non-return valves, windkessel aorta, venous return). Normal at 75 bpm: EDV ≈ 120 mL, ESV ≈ 50 mL, SV ≈ 70 mL, BP ≈ 120/80. The ECG is synthetic and timed to the model's activation.

    A valve decides nothing: blood only moves from higher to lower pressure, so each valve opens and shuts at the instant two pressures cross. The ventricle's squeeze and relaxation just set when they cross.

    Teaching model, not a patient simulator: lumped left heart (time-varying elastance ventricle and atrium, pressure-driven valves, elastic aorta, fixed blood volume). The heart is drawn in anatomical position with all four chambers; only the left heart (LA, LV, mitral and aortic valves, aorta) is simulated, so the right atrium, right ventricle and their valves are drawn still. Proportions are approximate. pressures, volumes, flows and timings come from the model (normal: 120/50 mL, 123/82 mmHg at 75 bpm).

    A valve decides nothing: blood only moves from higher to lower pressure, so each valve opens and shuts at the instant two pressures cross. The ventricle's squeeze and relaxation just set when they cross.

    Teaching model, not a patient simulator: lumped left heart (time-varying elastance ventricle and atrium, pressure-driven valves, elastic aorta, fixed blood volume). The RV and pulmonary artery are anatomical context and are drawn still. Shapes are stylised; pressures, volumes, flows and timings come from the model (normal: 120/50 mL, 123/82 mmHg at 75 bpm).

The big idea: a valve decides nothing. Blood only flows from higher to lower pressure, so each valve flips at the instant two pressures cross: "lub" when the squeezing ventricle overtakes the atrium, "dub" when the relaxing ventricle falls below the aorta.

COLOUR

Teaching model, not a patient simulator. Pressures, volumes, flows and timings come from a lumped left-heart model (normal 120/50 mL, 123/82 mmHg at 75 bpm); The heart is drawn in anatomical position with all four chambers; only the left heart (LA, LV, mitral and aortic valves, aorta) is simulated, so the right atrium, right ventricle and their valves are drawn still. Proportions are approximate. Sound: S1 = mitral + tricuspid closure, S2 = aortic + pulmonary closure (split), with murmurs for the valve conditions; synthesised, timed by the model; headphones recommended. Keys 1–6 play a step, space plays the whole beat, M mutes or unmutes, drag the heart to rotate.

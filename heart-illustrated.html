The big idea: a valve decides nothing. Blood only flows from higher to lower pressure, so each valve flips at the instant two pressures cross: "lub" when the squeezing ventricle overtakes the atrium, "dub" when the relaxing ventricle falls below the aorta.

COLOUR

Teaching model, not a patient simulator. Pressures, volumes, flows and timings come from a lumped left-heart model (normal 120/50 mL, 123/82 mmHg at 75 bpm); shapes are stylised; the right ventricle and pulmonary artery are drawn still. Keys 1–6 play a step, space plays the whole beat, drag the heart to rotate.
